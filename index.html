01 · Royalty model

Commercial partners sell the products. TherapeuticsMD earns on every sale.

Since December 30, 2022, TherapeuticsMD has licensed its products to pharmaceutical companies with commercial capabilities in each territory, and receives royalties and sales-based milestones in return.

  1. 01LicenseExclusive, perpetual licenses: Mayne Pharma for the United States, Theramex outside the U.S. except Canada, Knight in Canada.
  2. 02CommercializeThe licensees manufacture, market and sell IMVEXXY, BIJUVA, ANNOVERA and the prenatal vitamin lines.
  3. 03CollectTherapeuticsMD collects royalties and milestone payments. In 2026 to date, 100% of license revenue came from these three licensees.

IMVEXXY®

estradiol vaginal inserts, 4-µg and 10-µg

For moderate-to-severe dyspareunia (vaginal pain associated with sexual activity), a symptom of vulvar and vaginal atrophy due to menopause.

  • Mayne Pharma · U.S.
  • Theramex · International
  • Knight · Canada

BIJUVA®

estradiol and progesterone capsules, 1 mg/100 mg

The first and only FDA approved bioidentical hormone therapy combination of estradiol and progesterone in a single, oral capsule, for moderate-to-severe hot flashes due to menopause in women with a uterus.

  • Mayne Pharma · U.S.
  • Theramex · International
  • Knight · Canada

ANNOVERA®

segesterone acetate and ethinyl estradiol vaginal system

A one-year contraceptive ring: the first and only patient-controlled, procedure-free, reversible prescription contraceptive that can prevent pregnancy for up to 13 cycles.

  • Mayne Pharma · U.S.

Also licensed to Mayne Pharma: prescription prenatal vitamins under the vitaMedMD® and BocaGreenMD® brands. Product logos as shown on therapeuticsmd.com; trademarks of their owners. Descriptions from the 2025 Form 10-K.

03 · Licensees

Three licensees, three territories

Each partner holds an exclusive license for its territory and pays TherapeuticsMD on its sales.

  • United StatesMayne PharmaSubsidiary of Mayne Pharma Group Limited, an Australian public company. Licensed IMVEXXY, BIJUVA and the prenatal vitamins, and took assignment of ANNOVERA, on December 30, 2022.
  • Outside the U.S. and CanadaTheramexLicense for IMVEXXY and BIJUVA since June 2019; secured approval for BIJUVA in certain European countries in 2021. Israel rights transferred to Theramex in December 2024.
  • CanadaKnight TherapeuticsLicense for IMVEXXY and BIJUVA since July 2018; obtained regulatory approval and began commercialization in 2024.
04 · Milestones

A royalty term that runs to 2042

The Mayne Pharma royalty runs for 20 years from closing. In 2026, TherapeuticsMD and Mayne Pharma settled the IMVEXXY patent cases with Sun Pharma and Teva, which set the dates their generics may enter the U.S. market.

Both settlements are subject to court approval, and each allows earlier entry under certain circumstances. The IMVEXXY patents named in the cases expire in 2032 or 2033. Source: 10-Q, Nov 13, 2026.

  1. Dec 30, 2022Mayne transaction closes$140.0M in cash at closing; the U.S. royalty term begins.
  2. 2024Launch in CanadaKnight obtains approval and begins commercializing IMVEXXY and BIJUVA.
  3. Dec 2024Israel to TheramexRights for IMVEXXY and BIJUVA in Israel move from Knight to Theramex.
  4. Sep and Oct 2026IMVEXXY settlementsSettlement and license agreements with Sun Pharma (Sep 29) and Teva (October).
  5. Sep 2, 2032Earliest settled generic entryTeva from Sep 2, 2032; Sun Pharma from Oct 21, 2032.
  6. Dec 2042Royalty term ends20 years from closing; the Mayne license then becomes fully paid-up.
